How I work

Three things I do on every project.

Enterprise work rewards discipline over flourish. This is the discipline.

01

Start with the people, not the data

Interviews, personas, journey maps and empathy work come first — so the interface is built around the decision a broker, rep or analyst is actually trying to make.

02

Turn requirements into an architecture

I translate messy, competing requirements into a clear information architecture and flows — securing stakeholder buy-in by aligning specs with real user needs before a pixel is drawn.

03

Ship a system, not screens

High-fidelity, dev-ready designs backed by a reusable design system — so what I hand off stays consistent, scales, and survives contact with engineering.
